# Content type: how-to

This page teaches the reader to perform a task. That changes two of the answers
above.

**`intent.format` is `how-to guide`.** Rule 4 has you read the format from the
competitor pages, and for a task query those pages are usually how-tos already,
so this often agrees with them. Set it explicitly anyway, because a retail page
in the research can otherwise pull the format toward a product page.

**Prefer the term that asks how to do the thing.** Rule 2 still decides on demand
and fit, and fit here means the searcher is mid-task: "how to ...", "how often
to ...", "... not working", "cleaning ...". A bare product term is served better
by a guide or a roundup, so among terms that fit equally, prefer the one phrased
as the task, and say in `rationale` that the how-to format decided the tie.
