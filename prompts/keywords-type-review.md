# Content type: single-product review

This page reviews one product the author has personally used. That changes three
of the answers above, and nothing else.

**`intent.format` is `review`.** Rule 4 tells you to read the format from the
competitor pages. For a product name those pages are retail listings and
manufacturer pages, and they rank because they sell the product, not because
they answer the searcher. A review does not copy their format. Read
`intent.type` and `intent.angle` from the competitors as rule 4 says, and set
`intent.format` to `review`.

**The primary keyword is the product itself.** A review can only honestly target
the thing the author used, so the highest-scoring term does not win here if it
names a different product, a variant the author did not own, or a category. Pick
the plainest name for the reviewed product among the terms in the research, and
let the qualified variants be secondary. Say in `rationale` that the product
decided the choice.

**`must_cover` is what a buyer needs, not only what the retail pages share.**
Rule 7 still bounds you to the research, and ownership subtopics inside it count:
what it costs to keep running, what wears out, what goes wrong. Include those
when the research carries them, even where no competitor page covers them, since
that absence is the gap a review fills.
