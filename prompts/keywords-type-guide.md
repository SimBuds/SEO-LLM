# Content type: buying guide

This page teaches the reader how to choose, and names no product. That changes
two of the answers above.

**`intent.format` is `ultimate guide`.** Rule 4 has you read the format from the
competitor pages, and for a commercial term those pages are usually retail
listings and product roundups. A guide does not copy their format. Read
`intent.type` and `intent.angle` from the competitors as rule 4 says, and set
`intent.format` to `ultimate guide`.

**`must_cover` are criteria, not products.** Rule 7 still bounds you to subtopics
the research carries, but state each one as the thing the reader weighs rather
than as a competitor's heading. A competitor's "Our Top Picks" is not a subtopic
this page can cover, and copying it commits the article to picks it cannot make.
Where the research offers only that, use the criterion underneath it: what the
picks are being judged on.
