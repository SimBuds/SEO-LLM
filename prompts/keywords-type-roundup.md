# Content type: product roundup

This page recommends specific products the author has tested. That changes two
of the answers above.

**`intent.format` is `listicle`.** Rule 4 has you read the format from the
competitor pages, and a roundup's competitors are usually roundups, so this
often agrees with them. Set it explicitly anyway, because a retail category page
in the research can otherwise pull the format toward a product page.

**Prefer a term a roundup can honestly satisfy.** Rule 2 still decides on demand
and fit, and fit here means the searcher is choosing between products: "best
<thing>", "<thing> reviews", "top <thing>" and comparative variants. A bare
category term is served better by a guide, so among terms that fit equally,
prefer the one that asks for a recommendation, and say in `rationale` that the
roundup format decided the tie.
